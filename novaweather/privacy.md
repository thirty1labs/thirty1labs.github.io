# NovaWeather - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 08/14/26

NovaWeather is developed by Noah Novak, operating as Thirty1 Labs, a non-U.S. registered business.

## 1. Data We Collect

NovaWeather does not collect, store, or share personal information.

## 2. Location Data

The App may request your device's location to show local weather forecasts. This location is used only to fetch weather data from weather.gov (and/or Apple WeatherKit) and is not stored or transmitted to us or any third party beyond what's needed to complete that request.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Weather data is provided by:

- **weather.gov** (National Weather Service, a U.S. government service; see <https://www.weather.gov/privacy> for privacy information)
- **Open-Meteo** (for non-U.S. users; see <https://open-meteo.com/en/terms> for privacy information)

These providers may have their own data practices, which are outside our control. See their respective policies for details.

## 4. Analytics & Tracking

NovaWeather will collect analytics and diagnostics if users enable "Share with App Developers" in the "Analytics & Improvements" section in the iOS settings or similar settings, where available, on other platforms, such as Android. These consist of crash logs only, with the sole purpose being to improve issues. This is an optional feature that can be turned off at any time. Any logs shared with the developer are completely anonymous, meaning no user information, like locations, names, etc, will be shared with the developer or sold to third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

NovaWeather does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App develops. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
